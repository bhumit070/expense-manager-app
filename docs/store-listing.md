# Store listing — Expense Manager

## Title
Expense Manager — Offline Budget

## Short description (≤ 80 chars)
Track spending, budgets, investments and debts. Private, offline, no account.

## Full description
Expense Manager keeps your money in one clear picture — and on your phone only.

There is no account, no server and no sync. Every transaction, budget, investment and debt is stored locally in an on-device database. Export to CSV or PDF whenever you like.

**Track everything**
• Spend, Invest, Income and Debt — investments are reported separately from spending, so your "spent" number stays honest
• Categories with a default "Others", payment sources with a default payment mode and running balances
• Notes and #tags on every transaction, all searchable
• Optional location tagging (only if you allow it)

**Understand it**
• Monthly and yearly insights: spend vs invest, savings rate, category breakdowns, week-by-week bars
• Weekly (Mon–Sun) and monthly (1st–end) budgets with pace markers, rollover and investment goals

**Investments and debts — just the numbers**
• Record what you put into FDs, mutual funds, stocks, gold and PPF. No live prices, no tracking — just the amounts you invested
• Loans with EMIs, credit cards, money lent and borrowed

**Your data, your files**
• CSV import with duplicate detection; CSV and PDF export
• Light, dark and system themes
