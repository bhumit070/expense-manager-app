# Privacy policy — Expense Manager

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

**Expense Manager does not collect any data.**

- **Everything stays on your device.** Transactions, categories, payment sources, budgets, investments, debts and settings are stored in a local database inside the app's private storage. There is no account, no server, no cloud sync and no analytics or advertising SDK.
- **Location is optional.** If you turn on "Location on transactions" and grant permission, the app reads your current position when you add a transaction and stores the place name and coordinates with that transaction, on your device. The device's built-in geocoder may be used to turn coordinates into a place name. You can turn this off at any time in Settings or in your phone's settings.
- **Files you choose.** Import reads only the file you pick. Export creates a CSV or PDF and hands it to the share sheet; where it goes next is up to you.
- **Error log.** Crashes and errors are written to a log file on your device only. It is never sent anywhere; you can view, share or clear it in Settings → Diagnostics.
- **Deleting your data.** Uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Clearing the app's storage does the same.
- **Children.** The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Data safety (Play Console): _No data collected · No data shared · Data is not encrypted in transit (no data is transmitted) · Users can request deletion by uninstalling._
App privacy (App Store Connect): _Data Not Collected._
